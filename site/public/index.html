Crew

A desktop app for the coding agent CLIs you already pay for: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and opencode.

Run a roster of bots, each with its own chat. They can message each other and keep working on a schedule.

Everything runs on your machine: no account, no sync, no keys of ours. Connect the services you use, like your Google apps or Notion, and choose which tools your agents may use.
